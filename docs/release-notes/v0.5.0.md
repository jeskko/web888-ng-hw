**The channelizer for the VHF input** (`websdr_pfb_vhf.bit`,
`docs/channelizer.md` §3.10). Signature `0x0E5D2110`.

> **VHF still needs testing against real signals or a signal
> generator.** It has run on a Web-888 with nothing on its VHF
> connector: the clock, the bitstream, the RF switch and the data path
> work, but no VHF signal has been received with it yet. Levels,
> sensitivity, the input filter's effect (what else folds into the band)
> and audio quality on real stations are unknown.

* **VHF by undersampling.** With the ADC clocked at 98.304 MHz (the
  stock firmware's airband clock) and the RF switch on the VHF input,
  98.304-147.456 MHz (airband, 2 m) lands in the ADC's first Nyquist
  zone the right way up. The same channelizer splits it into 24 kHz
  bins at 48 kS/s; the power line, the zoom engine and the bin selector
  work as on HF.
* **The tuner** decimates by 4 instead of 5 at this clock, so its 32
  receivers still give 12 kS/s; its filter is designed for 48 kS/s
  (0.0005 dB ripple, 87 dB stopband).
* **The signature says the clock** the bitstream is built for (bits
  [11:8]: 0 HF, 1 VHF), so the server can refuse a bitstream that
  doesn't match the programmed clock. The driver is unchanged; the
  tuner's `RX_START` takes 40 at either clock.
* **Built** with the VHF build's constraints (9.0 ns plus a 0.5 ns
  guardband, enough for 110.592 MHz): WNS +0.456 ns, 77.5 % LUT, 59 of
  60 block RAMs, 71 of 80 DSP slices.
* **Simulated**: the tuner bit-exact to its model at both decimations
  (`fpga/scripts/tune_model.py`), the whole build through its registers
  at 98.304 MHz (`make sim-core-pfb`).
* **On a Web-888 (2026-11-12)**, with web888d's `band = "vhf"`
  (web888-ng v0.10.0), a few minutes and later one morning as the
  service: the Si5351 at 98.304 MHz, RX at 12 035 frames/s (the clock
  confirmed), a flat noise floor apart from the ADC's offset in the
  first bin and one spur 3 dB above the floor (125.004 MHz), the HF
  antenna's strongest stations more than 55 dB down (the RF switch on
  VHF), zoomed views on the engine. Switching back to HF restored the
  HF clock and reception as before.
* `websdr_pfb.bit` (HF channelizer), `websdr_hf.bit`, `websdr_vhf.bit`:
  rebuilt from this tag, their function as in v0.4.0 (the HF
  channelizer's tuner is unchanged, only parameterised).

Use it on a unit you can't power-cycle only with the watchdog armed, as in
`docs/hardware-test-plan.md`.
